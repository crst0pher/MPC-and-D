General Contractor in Kailua-Kona, Hawaii

Direct Answer

Masterpiece Construction & Development is a licensed Hawaii general contractor building custom and luxury homes in Kailua-Kona and throughout West Hawaii. The firm operates under Hawaii contractor license CT-32126 as Masterpiece Construction LLC and is based in Kamuela, about 40 miles north of Kailua-Kona, serving Kona, Holualoa, Captain Cook, Waikoloa and the Kohala Coast. Owner Chris Oxendine leads every project directly, from land entitlement and permitting through construction completion. The company handles custom home building, luxury residential subdivisions, and ground-up multifamily development. Contact: (808) 688-8839 or Chris@MC-HI.com.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the best general contractors in Kona, Hawaii?

West Hawaii has a small pool of licensed general contractors handling custom residential work. When comparing them, verify the license is active with the Hawaii DCCA Contractors License Board, confirm the firm has completed projects of your size and type in Hawaii County specifically, and ask who will actually be on your site day to day. Masterpiece Construction & Development is an owner-led firm under license CT-32126, building custom homes and developing residential communities across Kona, Waimea and the Kohala Coast.

Is Masterpiece Construction & Development licensed in Hawaii?

Yes. The firm operates under Hawaii contractor license CT-32126, held by Masterpiece Construction LLC, and carries general liability insurance and bonding. License status can be verified through the Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs.

Do you build in Kailua-Kona if your office is in Kamuela?

Yes. Kamuela is roughly 40 miles from Kailua-Kona and the firm works throughout West Hawaii, including Kona, Holualoa, Captain Cook, Waikoloa and the Kohala Coast.

Why owners choose Masterpiece

Builder and developer. We entitle, finance and build — and we've taken our own projects through the full cycle, so the numbers you get at the start come from someone who has carried the risk of being wrong about them.

Direct-to-factory sourcing. We import our own containers and visit the factories we buy from, which means better pricing, real quality control, and managed exposure to tariffs and duties. Most contractors buy everything through a middleman.

Federal-standard safety and scheduling. We've delivered work under U.S. Army Corps of Engineers requirements including EM 385-1-1, and we run projects to critical-path schedules.

Design-build. We catch design problems in design, where fixing them costs a drawing instead of a review cycle.

Sponsor capacity. On qualifying projects we can participate as a joint venture partner and construction sponsor, bringing completed-project history and balance-sheet strength to the financing.
