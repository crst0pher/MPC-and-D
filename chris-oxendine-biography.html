Overview

Chris Oxendine is a Hawaii real estate developer and general contractor, and the founder and principal of Masterpiece Construction & Development, a design-build and development firm established in 2010.

Oxendine's work spans the full development cycle: land acquisition and entitlement, design, construction, and sale. Under his direction the firm builds custom and luxury residences, develops residential subdivisions and multifamily communities, and takes on land development and specialty projects across Oahu, the Big Island and Maui, with development activity on the U.S. mainland.

He has more than 20 years of residential and development experience on Oahu. Much of that came through large-scale work for Hawaii's privatized military housing operators, where Masterpiece served as a select general contractor to Hunt Companies, Lendlease and ForestCity/Northstar across portfolios totaling more than 20,000 homes.
